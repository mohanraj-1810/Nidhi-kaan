The Problem
1.

Manual inspection is slow.

Physical officer visits take weeks, creating systemic bottlenecks in fund sanction and milestone sign-offs across remote taluks.

2.

Evidence is easy to fake.

Unverified geotags, reused completion photos from previous fiscal years, and synthetic imagery bypass visual scrutiny.

3.

Delays have no consequence.

Stalled verification files sit on field desks without automated hierarchy alerts, breaking accountability chains.

Scheme Use Cases

Nidhi Kaan is scheme-agnostic — built once, deployable across any Tamil Nadu government fund-utilization scenario.

Use Case A

Housing Scheme

Kalaignarin Kanavu Illam / PMAY-style

Beneficiaries receive installment funds to build a house. Each stage (foundation → walls → roof → finishing) requires a geo-tagged photo before the next tranche is released. Staff visits are also photo-verified to close the inspector corruption loop.

Geo-tagged stage-by-stage photo verification
Auto-notice on missed submission deadline
Inspector visit photo accountability loop
Use Case B

Road Repair

Pothole & Road Damage Verification

Citizens crowdsource road damage complaints with geo-tagged before-photos. On contractor completion, an after-photo is submitted. AI compares before vs. after imagery at the exact GPS coordinates to verify real repair — not a recycled photo.

Crowdsourced citizen complaint intake
Before/after computer vision comparison
GPS-locked location matching
Use Case C

Bus Stand / Infrastructure

MAWS Dept Funded Public Works

Staged infrastructure construction (bus stands, municipal buildings) verified milestone-by-milestone. Material procurement bills cross-checked against GST records. Fund tranches released only when AI confirms genuine progress on ground.

Milestone-gated fund tranche release
GST bill cross-check on material claims
Active pilot target: Tirupur Bus Stand
AI அமைப்புகள்
Four-Layer Core AI Architecture

Each layer functions as an independent AI subsystem feeding a common escalation engine — any module can independently detect fraud and trigger auto-escalation.

AI System 01

Authenticity & Fraud Detection AI

Gatekeeper — rejects fakes, duplicates, and spoofed coordinates

Geotag/EXIF
GPS coordinates matched to assigned cadastral site boundaries
Forced In-App Camera
Gallery uploads blocked — only direct camera sensor stream accepted
pHash Duplicate Filter
Perceptual hashing catches reused photos across projects & years
ELA / Tamper Net
Error Level Analysis & CNN detect spliced or AI-generated imagery
Failure Result: Submission auto-rejected, Tamil notice sent, audit logged
AI System 02

Progress & Damage Understanding AI

Computer vision verifying actual structural completion against claims

Stage Classifier
CNN classifies stage: Foundation → Walls → Lintels → Slab Roof → Plaster
Road Condition Model
Classifies asphalt surface state: Severe Pothole → Patched → Sealed
Multi-Temporal Comparative Engine
Compares latest photo with historical site baseline to calculate actual delta progress percentage — prevents static/unworked scenes from triggering tranches
Architecture: Fine-tuned EfficientNet-B4 + Mask R-CNN segmentor
AI System 03

Financial & GST Bill Verification AI

Direct GSTN reconciliation & physical quantity cross-check

GSTIN Live Verification
Validates supplier status, active filing history, and registration zone
GSTR-2B Cross-Match
Confirms invoice was officially reported by vendor in government portal
Quantity vs Site AI
Compares billed steel/cement tons against System 2 structural volume
Duplicate Invoice Registry
Global cryptographic invoice hash stops recycling bills across departments
Mismatch: Auto-holds disbursement + files tax inquiry alert
AI System 04

Notice & Auto-Escalation Engine

Autonomous SLA state machine — removes human discretion bottlenecks

SLA Countdown Timers
Deterministic timers for inspections, reviews, and authority clearances
Native Tamil Notices
Templated statutory notices generated and sent via SMS/Push automatically
Unstoppable Escalation
If a desk stalls, case auto-advances to District Collector, State, and CM
Dual-Sided Proof
Inspectors must submit their own verified geo-photos when visiting sites
Chain: Local Staff (24h) → District (48h) → State (72h) → CM Dashboard

AI System 05 — Predictive Risk Scoring (Proactive Layer)

Moving from reactive penalties to proactive prevention. Classical explainable ML calculates real-time risk scores for contractors, field inspectors, and districts to prevent fraud before public funds move.

01 • Behavioral Feature Engineering

Aggregates historical SLA breach frequencies, invoice mismatch rates, photo re-take counts, and geo-fence deviation radii into dynamic feature vectors.

02 • Explainable Gradient Boosting

Uses auditable XGBoost / LightGBM models. Every score comes with SHAP explainability values so officers understand why an entity was flagged.

03 • Automatic Preventive Safeguards

Entities with risk >70 trigger mandatory dual-officer physical inspections, narrower SLA deadlines, and secondary GST scrutinies before tranche approvals.
